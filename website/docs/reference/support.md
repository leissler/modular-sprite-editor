# Get help

Start with [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md); many questions have a short answer there.

To report a problem or suggest an improvement, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/leissler/modular-sprite-editor/issues). You can also write to [info@bionic-productions.com](mailto:info@bionic-productions.com).

Tell us:

- what you wanted to make, and what happened instead;
- the shortest steps that show it;
- the release you downloaded, for example 0.6, and your system: Windows, macOS or Linux;
- a screenshot, and if you can, a small project that shows the problem.

A small project with one image and a few frames is much easier to look at than a whole game. Only share artwork you are allowed to share.

Questions about this guide are welcome too. Include the page and the step that was unclear.
